## ACT II. THE RICANTO SCANDAL : 800 MILLION EUROS FOR A RAPESEED MIRAGE

To justify the continued payment of this rent and bury promised natural gas pipelines, the state and EDF imposed the **Ricanto Project** (130 MW), greenwashed under the « bioliquids » label.

Autopsy of the instruction records, the **Prefectoral Order of February 15, 2024**, and **EDF PEI's legal brief** dismantles this communication:

1. **The €800 Million Financial Heist**: Initially estimated at €450M, capital expenditure exploded to reach **€800 Million including VAT** to mount 8 giant diesel engines (MAN 18V48/60).
2. **The Larivot Precedent in French Guiana (Note 2.2 p. 16 of 2025 Accounts)**:
   * EDF PEI applies the same legal playbook as at Larivot in Guiana, where thermal bioliquid works were suspended by administrative courts before being pushed through with 12.8 km of pipelines and imported fuel tanks.
3. **€902.9 Million in Fuels Swallowed in a Single Year (Note 7 p. 17)**:
   * Certified accounts disclose fuel consumption of **€902.9 Million in 2025** (+€26.4M in one year). This torrent of public money enriches fuel trading cartels rather than funding local solar and hydro autonomy.
4. **EDF PEI's Written Admission (MRAe Brief p. 7-9)**: EDF PEI admits in writing abandoning the combustion turbine (TAC) to add an **8th giant diesel engine**, designing engines to burn **Light Domestic Fuel (FOD)** as backup.
5. **Captive Rent of the Saipol / Avril Group Cartel**: Corsica possesses zero rapeseed fields. The decree of **June 30, 2023** chains the island to maritime imports of **50,000 tons annually of refined rapeseed oil** transported by cargo ships from the mainland, creating total logistical vulnerability.
6. **Official Admission of Carbon Heavy Footprint (TPE 2026, p. 26)**: The state and EDF acknowledge that Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of imported rapeseed generates **252 g CO2eq / kWh**, drastically heavier than insular hydro or solar power.
7. **Hazardous Site Location (Prefectoral Order of Feb 15, 2024 & MRAe Opinion)**: Order No. `2A-2024-02-15-00002` confirms location in a **low-lying flood zone exposed to marine submersion**, adjacent to Ajaccio's new hospital and Seveso facilities.
8. **Lapse Clause (Page 40 of Prefectoral Order)**: The order mandates a 5-year lapse period (February 2029) suspended in the event of administrative court litigation!
9. **Verdict of the Bufitonu.fr Radar**: Cross-referencing cadastre data and permits proves this 130 MW plant is calibrated not for the year-round needs of 350,000 residents (who consume 80-100 MW in the south), but to **guarantee summer air conditioning peaks for tourism real estate speculation**.

## ACT V. THE SAINT-ANTOINE DIVERSION VS TRUE HYDROELECTRIC SOVEREIGNTY

### ☣️ Forensic Autopsy of Saint-Antoine : Textbook Financial & Ecological Heist
To divert public anger, Ajaccio municipality and state agencies promoted the private **Saint-Antoine project (Ajaccio / Alata)**, attributed to **EDF Power Solutions (13 MW)** as confirmed during the **Corsica Ecological COP of July 2, 2026**.

Forensic auditing of the litigation brief before Bastia Administrative Court (*Foata v. City of Ajaccio*), supported by *Bufitonu.fr* data, exposes 5 fatal locks:

1. **Geotechnical Peril on Landfill Site (10.5 Hectares of Industrial Waste)**:
   * The site is an unstable closed landfill generating methane and toxic leachate.
   * Driving thousands of metallic foundation piles threatens to puncture the HDPE capping membrane, allowing rainwater infiltration that would **drain lethal leachate loaded with heavy metals straight into the Gravona River** (Ajaccio's drinking water intake).
   * The Prefecture was forced to issue a **formal opposition order under the Water Act**.

2. **Tax Engineering : Dedicated Shell Entity & 30% CIIC Corporate Credit**:
   * Why create **SAS Centrale Photovoltaïque de Saint-Antoine** (a shell without staff) instead of contracting directly with parent company *EDF Renouvelables*?
   * **Mechanism documented by the Regional Court of Accounts (CRC)**: The CRC denounces total opacity surrounding the **actual beneficial ownership (RBE)** of local vehicles capturing territorial subsidies.
   * **Risk of tax abuse (CIIC - Art. 244 quater E CGI)**: The Corsican Tax Credit refunds **30% of capital expenditure** exclusively to genuine independent island SMEs to offset insularity costs. Channeling funds through a local shell owned by national holdings to siphon credits constitutes an artificial arrangement sanctioned by the Council of State (*CE, March 21, 2018, Société Serco*).

3. **Public Domain Spoliation & Prohibited Gift (€32 Million Annuity)**:
   * The plant will generate **20 GWh/year**, or **€32 Million in revenue over 20 years** guaranteed by the public CSPE.
   * The municipality concessions 10.5 ha of public land for a derisory fee without public tender, violating **Article L. 2122-1-1 CGPPP** and jurisprudence against unlawful public subsidies (*CE, Nov 3, 2010, Ville de Paris*).

4. **Normative Hierarchy Conflict : APER National Decree vs Regional PADDUC**:
   * The site is classified under the regional PADDUC as protected **Strategic Agricultural Land (ESA)** and ecological corridors (strictly non-buildable).
   * The state is attempting to force through an exceptional national decree (APER Act), violating the exclusive spatial planning powers of the Assembly of Corsica and Coastal Law rules (*TA Bastia, No. 2000110*).

5. **Regional Duplication & The Imperative for a Global Fiscal Audit**:
   * This model of deploying solar on landfills via project shells is being replicated at **Tallone (Plaine Orientale)**, industrial brownfields in **Borgo / Lucciana / Corte (+100 MW ground-mounted)**, and **Viggianello (Valinco)**.
   * **Citizen Demand**: L'OCHJU demands an independent fiscal audit of all beneficial ownership filings (RBE) and CIIC claims submitted by these subsidiaries.
   * **The Systemic Trap**: By saturating the grid with ground-mounted megaprojects, the monopoly triggers the **Article 22 lock (30% cap)** to **block the grid integration of 180 MW of clean citizen and agricultural rooftop solar** mapped by *Bufitonu.fr*.
