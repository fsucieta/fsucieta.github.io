## Bastia Moment of Truth: Two Hundred Helmets on the Tarmac

Bastia, Lupino Fire Station, September 28, 2026: two hundred silver F1 helmets laid down in silence before marching to the Prefecture under one banner: **"The State collects the tourist revenue, the Corsican people pay for the firefighters."**

On July 24–25, 2026, during extreme heat and 110 km/h Libecciu winds, French Civil Protection came within hours of collapse: **only 5 out of 12 CL-415 Canadairs were airworthy**, while seven remained grounded in Nîmes-Garons due to wing-spar cracks and spare-parts shortages. Earlier in winter 2025, 230 hectares burned at Barbaggio on January 3 with zero water-bombers stationed on the island.

## Autopsy of Structural Asymmetry: 355,528 Residents vs. 3 Million Tourists

### The TSCA Legal Lock: Frozen on Year-2003 Vehicles
Ministerial Reply No. 3779 reveals Corsica's €7.76M TSCA insurance tax allocation is legally frozen on the number of vehicles registered on **December 31, 2003** (Finance Act 2005, Art. 53) — rendering tens of thousands of summer rental cars and motorhomes invisible.

## Confiscated Skies: Nîmes Monopoly, €52.8M Cuts & The Olbia Ban

- **Balearic Islands (4,992 km²)**: 8 regional firefighting aircraft (1 per 500 km², <12 min strike).
- **Sardinia (24,100 km²)**: 17 regional helicopters + 3 Canadairs permanently stationed at **Olbia** — just **54 km (14–15 minutes flight)** from Bonifacio.
- **Corsica (8,680 km²)**: **ZERO regional aircraft**; **1h30–2h15** flight from mainland Nîmes-Garons; **€52.8M cancelled** by decree on February 21, 2024 (Program 161); and a Parliament-mandated Olbia cooperation report buried since June 28, 2023.
- **Sabena Technics Conflict of Interest**: Aircraft maintenance (~€30M/yr through 2027) is held exclusively by Sabena Technics FNI, co-owned by Sagard/TowerBrook alongside the French State itself via **Bpifrance**.
- **Dragon 2A & 2B Misuse**: Senate Report No. 393 confirms ~60% of mountain rescue helicopter hours are diverted to inter-hospital SAMU medical transfers (€4,500–€5,500/flight hour).
- **Rural Ambulance Drain & Asbestos**: Rural firefighter ambulances (VSAV) cover private ambulance shortages for a €217 fixed reimbursement against €450–€600 actual cost, leaving 1h32–4h coverage blackouts (SDACR Decree 135/2023), while Lupino station suffers degraded asbestos. Meanwhile, ferrying 200–350 mainland seasonal firefighters costs **€3.5M–€6M per summer** — equal to **70 to 120 permanent full-time local jobs**.
- **The EPISC Trap**: Deliberation 25/102 AC kept both Ajaccio and Furiani headquarters and two CODIS alert centers open, wasting **€4.50M/year**.
