**€112,941,976.** That is the exact figure collected every year by global giant **Veolia** from Corsican household water bills and municipal budgets.

## 1. RAINFALL ABUNDANCE VS. MANUFACTURED THIRST

Between autumn rains and winter snowpack on Monte Cinto, Paglia Orba, Renoso, and Bavella, **8 to 10 billion m³ of freshwater** fall on Corsica every year. Yet **85 out of every 100 liters flow straight back to the sea** without public storage:
- **Sardinia** (drier climate, OEHC figures): **37 large public dams**, storing **1.8 billion m³** (**1,100 m³ per inhabitant**).
- **Corsica**: public reserves store only **76 million m³** (**322 m³ per inhabitant** — 3.5x less than Sardinia).

### Locked Until 2096 (Law No. 2026-554) & Refusal of Closed-Loop Pumped Storage (STEP)
By Law No. 2026-554 of June 29, 2026, Paris locked our four major reservoirs (Calacuccia, Tolla, Sampolo, Rizzanese) under EDF control **until 2096**. EDF refuses closed-loop pumped hydro (STEP) — which would pump turbined water back up at midday using excess solar power (284.6 MW) — preferring a guaranteed 9.55% profit (€6.3B over 20 years under CRE 2026-111) burning imported rapeseed oil at Ricanto ([see Investigation #18](/en/investigations/18-le-monopole-energetique-edf-sei/)).

2. **Private Profit, Taxpayer Capital**: In Porto-Vecchio, taxpayers funded the €44M *Capu di Padolu* plant and *Nota* reservoirs while SDEC/Veolia kept the billing monopoly.
3. **Unspent Renewal Funds**: Mandatory meter/pump replacement reserves sit in Veolia's bank accounts for 12 years instead of being invested in pipes.
4. **Porto-Vecchio Independent Audit (October 2026, Amendment No. 1)**: Revealed a missing wage index in Veolia's price-escalation formula, resulting in an **undue +15% overcharge on household water bills**, excessive financial margins, delayed meter replacements, and outdated GIS pipe maps (ordered delivered by June 30, 2028).

## 6. THE SOVEREIGN SOLUTION: RECLAIMING 175 MILLION M³ / YEAR & UNIFIED PUBLIC WATER (`RSEI`)

Recovering just **2% of Corsica's annual rainfall** (+175 million m³/year over 8 years, scored **89.2/100** technical feasibility) requires three decisive steps:

1. **Create the Unified Public Water Authority (`RSEI`)**: Terminate fault-ridden private contracts without compensation (CGCT Art. L. 2224-5 & Public Procurement Code Art. L. 3136-3), audit all 60 Veolia towns for the +15% formula flaw, and transfer every technician under Labor Code Art. L. 1224-1 — keeping 100% of revenues in Corsica.
2. **Fair Tiered Pricing**: **0 to 30 m³/yr FREE (€0.00)** for vital human needs; **€2.80/m³ cost-price** for families (saving €250–€315/yr); **+300% luxury surcharge (€8–€12/m³)** above 150 m³ for summer pools and villas.
3. **8-Year Zero-Concrete Engineering Plan (+175M m³/yr)**:
   - Repair leaking mains from 58% to 85% efficiency (**+27.8M m³/yr**).
   - Underground alluvial dams beneath gravel riverbeds & Eastern Plain irrigation reserves (**+59.0M m³/yr**).
   - Closed-loop pumped hydro (STEP) & in-pipe micro-turbines (**+40.0M m³/yr**).
   - Coastal submarine freshwater spring capture (**+33.2M m³/yr**) & deep-sea water cooling SWAC (**+15.0M m³/yr**).
