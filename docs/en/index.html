ENERGY & STRATEGIC COMMONS

The EDF Monopoly & the Colonial Rent System: The Thermal Power Plant Sham vs. Corsica's Resource Abundance

523 megawatts of clean insular capacity are already operational across Corsica: 220.7 MW of mountain hydro in our 4 public dams and 284.6 MW of solar energy harvested by 6,319 certified rooftops, farms, and workshops. Faced with this natural power, Corsica's all-time historical peak winter demand never exceeds 400 MW. The Vazzio and Ricanto thermal plants are technically obsolete and useless. Yet on June 29, 2026, the French State quietly passed a law locking our dams under a 70-year exclusive concession for EDF until 2096, capped citizen solar under an arbitrary 30% curtailment threshold, and imposed an €800 million thermal plant at Ricanto to burn 50,000 tons of continental rapeseed oil shipped by sea with a guaranteed 10% annual return paid from public utility taxes. While the State grants a guaranteed 9.75% return to private mainland conglomerates for a pumped-storage unit (STEP) in Ajaccio while acknowledging €350 million in net public savings, it forbids Corsicans from equipping their own public dams. Forensic autopsy of an organized colonial plunder.

💡 What is the "Citizen Right to Know"?

Under French and European public transparency law, no mayor, prefect, or public utility director can conceal public documents (budgets, building permits, water contracts, council deliberations). Every citizen has the strict legal right to demand unredacted copies. If an agency refuses, the official statutory watchdog is the CADA (Commission on Access to Administrative Documents).
