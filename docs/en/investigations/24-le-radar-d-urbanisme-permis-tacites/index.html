I. The Legal Mechanism of the Tacit Building Permit (R. 424-1)

In French urban planning law, the principle of « silence means agreement » applies to the review of building permit applications. Under Article R. 424-1 of the Urban Planning Code, if the competent planning authority (the municipal mayor or the state Prefect in RNU areas) fails to notify an express decision or an instruction extension within the statutory deadline of 2 or 3 months, the applicant automatically becomes the legal holder of a tacit building permit.

While initially introduced to protect citizens against administrative procrastination, this statutory provision has been converted on the Corsican coastline into an engineered legal weapon to pass controversial speculative developments away from public debate.

II. Bypassing Public Notice and Citizen Oversight

How does this administrative bypass function? A developer seeking to construct high-impact luxury villas in natural areas or coastal buffer zones submits an application during strategic calendar windows (just before summer breaks or end-of-year holidays). Planning instruction desks in municipal town halls or state DDTM offices, either overwhelmed or intentionally passive, allow the statutory 2-month review period to lapse without issuing a written refusal.

Once the tacit permit is legally acquired through the mere passage of time, the developer obtains a certificate of tacit authorization from the court clerk, then posts the on-site notice board during low season. By the time neighbors or environmental associations discover the construction site, the 2-month judicial appeal deadline has expired, rendering the permit unchallengeable.

« Nearly one in five building permits granted within remarkable coastal areas in Corsica is born through administrative silence. This is the urbanism of the fait accompli. »

III. Masked Data in the Sitadel2 Registry and Cadastre

Cross-referencing the national Sitadel2 database with parcel registries via our partner Bufitonu.fr reveals that tacit permits are systematically recorded with incomplete metadata: applicant holding structures conceal beneficial owners, precise floor areas are omitted, and public notice dates are left blank.

This opacity prevents the public from tracking the cumulative impact of coastal soil artificialization.

V. Legal Recommendations & Citizen FOIA (CADA) Actions on Planning Data

To restore urban planning transparency, citizens can execute four statutory FOIA petitions under Article L. 311-1 CRPA:

📌 CADA Action #1 : Demand Municipal Planning Application Deposit Registers

Demand from municipal town halls the certified registry of all building permits filed and transmission timestamps (Récépissés de Dépôt).

📌 CADA Action #2 : Demand Certificates of Tacit Authorization

Demand from municipal planning departments copies of all certificates of tacit approval (Certificats de Permis Tacite) issued over the past 36 months.

📌 CADA Action #3 : Demand DDTM Instruction Files on Coastal Applications

Request from the DDTM complete instruction worksheets regarding coastal building permits that received no formal decision.

VI. Forensic Analysis of Administrative Decrees & Urban Case Law

Scrutiny of tacit permit practices reveals deliberate administrative loopholes:

  1. Analysis of Incomplete Application Notifications : Planning desks intentionally omit issuing formal letters requesting missing documents, which would legally suspend the tacit approval clock.
  2. Scrutiny of On-Site Notice Board Evidence : Bailiff reports (Constats d’Huissier) certifying on-site public notice boards are executed in hidden locations not visible from public thoroughfares.
  3. Audit of Prefectoral Legality Control Reviews : Prefects routinely decline to exercise their right to challenge tacit permits before the administrative court, claiming lack of formal notification.

VII. Citizen FOIA (CADA) Recourse Framework for Planning Transparency

Under Article L. 311-1 CRPA, citizens can demand:

  1. Certified Municipal Permit Filing Ledgers (Mairies) : Date-stamped applications for all coastal zones.
  2. DDTM Planning Review Instructions (DDTM) : Memos recommending refusal that were never acted upon by executives.
  3. Environmental Authority Impact Screening Opinions (MRAe) : Negative screening requests granted to tacit projects.
  4. Water and Sanitation Network Connection Authorizations (Intercommunalities) : Approvals issued for tacit developments.

VIII. Institutional Actors & Networks of Influence Mapping

The investigation into Tacit Building Permits reveals key institutional entities:

  • Municipal Town Halls & Mayoral Planning Services : Exercising discretionary authority to issue or let permits become tacit.
  • DDTM (Direction Départementale des Territoires et de la Mer) : Instructing permits on behalf of rural communes without planning staff.
  • Speculative Real Estate Promoters & Land Speculators : Exploiting calendar loopholes to build in protected zones.
  • Environmental Defense Associations (U Levante, etc.) : Monitoring coastline sprawl and exposing unpublicized tacit construction sites.
  • Territorial Land Observatory (Bufitonu.fr) : Delivering open data transparency on building permits and cadastral changes.

Legislative Recommendations & Innovative Statutory Remedies :

  1. Statutory Abolition of Tacit Permits in Coastal and Protected Zones : Amend Article R. 424-1 of the Urban Planning Code to mandate that in coastal zones, protected natural spaces, and insular agricultural areas, administrative silence constitutes an express rejection (Le Silence Vaut Rejet).
  2. Mandatory Real-Time Digital Publication of All Permit Filings (Affichage Numérique Immédiat) : Legally require every municipality to publish all submitted building permit applications online on a public GIS map within 48 hours of receipt.
  3. Suspension of Judicial Appeal Clock Until Verified Construction Posting : Enact a statutory rule stipulating that the 2-month appeal deadline against any building permit begins only from the date of certified digital publication on the official regional portal, eliminating sham on-site notice tricks.\n
