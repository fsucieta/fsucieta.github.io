## 🔍 THE THREE PRIMARY DATA PIPELINES

1. **French Ministry of Finance Real Estate Data (DVF)**:  
   Tracking parcel-by-parcel sales prices, valuation spikes, and speculative land flipping across Corsica.
2. **INPI Beneficial Ownership Registries (RBE)**:  
   Unmasking complex corporate shell chains and identifying ultimate beneficial owners (UBO) behind anonymous holdings.
3. **European Space Agency (ESA) Copernicus Satellites**:  
   Multi-spectral orbital tracking of coastal urbanisation and land transformations over time.
