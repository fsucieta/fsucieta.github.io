### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY / SYNTHÈSE DE L'APPEL

Before the Liberty Bell rang in Philadelphia (1776), and before Enlightenment philosophers finished their treatises in Paris salons, a Mediterranean island nation promulgated the world's first written democratic constitution in modern history: **The 1755 Constitution of the Republic of Corsica**, drafted by General Pasquale Paoli, granting voting rights to women 165 years before the United States.

Twenty years later, the American Continental Army forged its very first official battle cry in Pennsylvania blood at the Battle of Paoli: **"REMEMBER PAOLI !"** (September 1777).

Today, faced with aggressive real estate speculation, legal denial of its native language, and the confiscation of its vital energy and water resources, Corsica breaks the silence of decades and appeals directly to the conscience of free nations and the United States Congress.
